# Data provenance

Checked: 2026-08-06

The component counts and base rules were checked against the 2024 Space Cowboys / Asmodee English rulebook and publisher product material:

- https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-asmodeeca/uploads/2022/01/SCSPL01EN_SPLENDOR_RULES_LIGHT.pdf
- https://www.spacecowboys.fr/splendor/details

These sources confirm 90 development cards (40 level 1, 30 level 2, 20 level 3), 10 noble tiles, five gold pieces, seven pieces of each gem color, and the base turn/end rules. They do not provide a convenient machine-readable transcription of all 90 card faces.

The numerical card and noble table was inherited from the v0.2 repository. During v0.3 it was transformed by color name into the single order:

`white, blue, green, red, black`

Stable IDs are generated by tier, bonus color, and per-color ordinal. Automated checks cover total and tier counts, per-tier bonus distribution, unique IDs, noble structure, and the two previously identified tier-2 corrections:

- white bonus, 2 points: green 1, red 4, black 2
- blue bonus, 2 points: white 2, red 1, black 4

Limitation: every individual face in the 90-card transcription was not independently verified against an official machine-readable list. The project therefore does not claim an independent card-by-card official audit beyond the checks documented above.
